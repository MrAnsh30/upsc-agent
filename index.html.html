UPSC Agent

UPSC Agent is a small personal automation that turns each day's current affairs into short revision videos for UPSC Civil Services aspirants. It collects news headlines from public RSS feeds, writes short summaries, keeps notes in a public Google Sheet, and publishes short videos to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. Videos are removed automatically after 10 days; the Google Sheet stays online.

The app is operated by its owner for personal, educational use. It is not offered to other users and does not ask other people to sign in.
