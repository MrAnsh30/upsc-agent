Privacy Policy

UPSC Agent is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. This policy explains what it does with data.

What the app accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel (via the YouTube API): to upload short videos and to delete those same videos after 10 days.
  • The owner's own Google Sheet (via the Google Sheets API): to store daily current-affairs notes.
  • Public news RSS feeds: to read publicly available headlines and summaries.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about any other person.
  • It does not ask visitors or viewers to sign in, and it does not read their data.
  • It does not use YouTube or Google user data for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are kept as private secrets in the owner's GitHub repository settings and are never published. The Google Sheet of study notes is intentionally public so students can revise from it; it contains news summaries only, not personal data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated from time to time. For questions, open an issue at github.com/MrAnsh30/upsc-agent/issues.
